# Results
35 policies x 10 simulated days each.

| | Baseline | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Commission | 20% | 28% |
| Peak incentive (Rs/hr) | 0 | 40 |
| Completion rate | 72.9% | 74.2% |
| Avg pickup ETA (min) | 4.43 | 4.28 |
| Driver take-home (Rs/hr) | 182 | 185 |
| Contribution / day (Rs) | 54,698 +/- 290 | 50,416 +/- 490 |
| Contribution / trip (Rs) | 19.4 | 17.6 |

- Feasible = driver take-home >= Rs 185/hr and completion >= 72%; 19 of 35 policies qualify.
- Baseline feasible? no.
- Fixing driver pay with a flat commission cut alone needs 15% commission and leaves Rs 37,896/day.
- Shifting pay into peak hours (28% + Rs 40/peak hour) meets the same floor and keeps Rs 12,521/day more (+33%); completion 74.2% vs 75.6%.
